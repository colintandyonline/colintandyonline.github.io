Prepare your technical brief

To help us understand your enquiry, include the following where relevant:

  • Your organisation and project objective
  • The existing technology or systems
  • Desired outcomes and important constraints
  • Indicative timescale and integration requirements

Please do not send passwords, API credentials or sensitive customer records in an initial enquiry.

What happens next?

We review your requirements and establish whether a technical discovery discussion is appropriate. Scope, access, testing and delivery arrangements are agreed separately.
