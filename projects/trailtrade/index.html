03 / Implementation

Engineering decisions and evidence

The reviewed public tree contains category, filtering, listing, seller and show cards plus scheduled-drop countdown components.

Reusable interface primitives such as buttons, cards, inputs and empty states demonstrate the component-based direction.

The full-project stack documents Next.js, Supabase and Stripe; the public edition focuses on selected front-end components, not the private commercial implementation.

04 / Scope and limitations

What the public evidence supports

Curated selected-source edition; some components are development placeholders. This is not a complete deployable marketplace, and no production transaction, livestream or performance claims are made.
