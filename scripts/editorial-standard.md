# CTDG editorial voice

Reference articles reviewed on 26 November 2026: https://ctdigital.group/blog/meet-cora-ai-receptionist , https://ctdigital.group/blog/automation-accountability , https://ctdigital.group/blog/business-data-before-ai and https://ctdigital.group/blog/connected-business-systems.

Write in the CTDG team's voice: practical British English, first-person plural, useful examples and plain explanations of why a technical choice matters. Start from the actual problem rather than a technology label. A little dry humour is welcome when appropriate; never make a claim more persuasive than its evidence. Explain missing information and ownership of the next decision without making public pages sound like internal release notes. Preserve technical and privacy limitations where they affect visitor decisions. Do not invent first-person founder anecdotes or attribute new statements to the founder. Keep official branding, visuals and approved film intact.
