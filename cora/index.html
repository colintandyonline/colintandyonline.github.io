YOUR QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Frequently asked questions

Explore Cora, our engineering capabilities and how we can work together.

What can Cora help me explore?

Use the guided tour to find engineering services, project case studies, developer documentation, interactive AI experiences and our contact centre.

Can I speak to Cora?

Yes. Open our interactive AI receptionist to try the corporate conversational experience. The engineering guide on this page uses selectable text topics.

What types of AI and automation can I explore?

Try workflow automation, lead follow-up and conversational reception in our Innovation Laboratory. Our engineering services include AI agents, integrations, custom applications and governance.

Can I inspect the technology behind your projects?

Yes. The project portfolios include architecture and technology information, while developer documentation links to reviewed public source and technical references.

Can I try an AI receptionist before discussing a project?

Yes. Explore the receptionist demonstration and voice library. Use fictional details when testing.

Can CTDG connect AI to my existing systems?

We can discuss your existing software, APIs, workflows and integration requirements. Feasibility and the required access are assessed during technical discovery.

Do you develop bespoke applications and AI agents?

Our engineering enquiry areas include custom web and mobile applications, AI agents, multi-agent systems and legacy modernisation. Tell us your intended outcomes so we can discuss the scope.

How do I get a quote or discuss my project?

Visit our engineering contact centre, email developers@ctdigital.group or use the existing CTDG enquiry form. Pricing and delivery are agreed after assessing your requirements.

Can developers collaborate with CTDG?

We welcome technical and research partnership enquiries. Explore our developer community, GitHub profile and contact centre.

What information should I share in an enquiry?

Describe the problem, intended outcomes and existing systems. Do not include passwords, API credentials or sensitive customer records in an initial enquiry. See our privacy policy.
