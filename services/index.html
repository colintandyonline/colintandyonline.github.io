Engineering questions, answered

How do we start an engineering engagement?

Contact CTDG with the problem, existing systems, intended outcomes and any security or integration requirements. A technical discovery discussion establishes feasibility and scope.

Are all these services available as fixed-price packages?

No. These are engineering enquiry categories. Delivery scope, availability, pricing and acceptance criteria are agreed after technical assessment.

Can we review your previous engineering work?

Yes. Our public portfolios link to reviewed source evidence, documented technology and project limitations. Selected public editions are not necessarily complete deployable applications.

Can you connect to our existing systems?

Integration feasibility depends on supported APIs, authentication, data contracts, access permission and the existing infrastructure. These are assessed before implementation.
