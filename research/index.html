How we classify technology

Documented stack means a technology is listed in our reviewed portfolio records. It does not certify production health. Evaluation proposed means a specific comparative experiment is suggested, not performed. Research candidate means we have identified an upstream source and a question to investigate. New claims require evidence; no private project data or live telemetry is exposed.
